Help & support

Common questions

Where are my files saved?

On this device, in your browser. The Free plan holds up to 5 MB. Clearing your browser data removes them, so export a backup from Files regularly.

What are Notes?

A place for your raw thinking and anything your AI tells you. Open Files, then tap Notes or a folder. Notes autosave and keep versions. They are separate from workspace files, and not in Context Pack or Export. Clearing browser data deletes them. Mitochondria only reads the notes you select. It never edits them, and nothing is sent anywhere until you copy the prompt.

What can I save on the Free plan?

Text only. Use Add file to type or paste. Uploading photos and files is for Pro.

How do versions work?

Saving with the same file name creates a new version (v1, v2, v3). If nothing changed, no new version is made.

How do I back up or move my files?

Open Files, then tap Export to download a backup, or Context Pack to export everything as one .md file. Import restores a backup.

I lost my license code

Tap Email support and include the email address you used. Guest codes (ARTI-FREE) are saved on this device only.

What happens when I paste long text?

Pasted paragraphs are split into rows of about 100 characters. Rows you type have no limit. The maximum is 50,000 lines per file.

Do members work across devices?

Not yet. Members and invite links are saved on this device until cloud sync is available.
